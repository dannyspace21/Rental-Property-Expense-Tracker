What do these mean?
  • Net operating income (NOI): income minus running costs (not counting mortgage, interest or big upgrades).
  • Cap rate: NOI ÷ property value. Compares properties as if bought with cash.
  • Cash-on-cash: the year's cash flow ÷ cash you've put in (down payment + closing costs + improvements).
  • Expense ratio: running costs as a share of income. Lower is better.
